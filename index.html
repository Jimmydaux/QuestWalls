Wall Sketch

Press START AR at the bottom to see your room through passthrough and draw walls on the floor.
  • Right trigger: place a corner (point at the floor)
  • Right grip: finish the wall run (left open)
  • A: close the shape (or click the first corner)
  • B: undo last corner / last shape
  • Left stick ↕: wall height · ↔: opacity
  • X: next color · Y: angle snap on/off
  • Left stick click: show/hide dimensions
  • Hold left grip 2s: clear everything
  • Right stick click with the controller touching the floor: set the floor height
Height, color, and opacity change the shape you're drawing. When you're not drawing, they change the last finished shape (outlined in yellow). To give a new shape a different color, place its first corner, then press X.
