Wall Sketch

Press START AR at the bottom. Your work saves automatically; use Layouts to keep named versions.

Modes (left trigger switches)

  • Walls: corners on the floor become walls. A ends the run; click the first corner to close a room
  • Doors: point at a wall, trigger one side, then the other (same spot twice = 36" door). Point at a door + B removes it
  • Furniture: corners become a solid block (3 corners + A makes a rectangle)
  • Items: realistic furniture (sofa, bed, table…); pick it in the menu. Right stick ↔ rotates, ↕ steps through items
  • Selecting, in any mode: hold the right grip and pull the trigger on something. Or while holding the grip, just point and press B to delete it. After selecting: A = done, B = delete, hold the trigger to move it; left stick ↔ width, ↕ depth; right stick ↔ rotate, ↕ height. (Select mode works the same without holding the grip)
  • Menu: press ☰ on the left controller to open it, again to close it: every mode, furniture, colors, view options and actions for the selection. Point the right laser and pull the trigger
  • Recalibrate floor height (menu): if things sit too high or low (e.g. after taking the headset off), choose it, then touch the controller to the floor and press A
  • Look only (menu): every button, stick and laser is off, so you can walk around or hand the headset over. ☰ still opens the menu
  • Anchor A/B: mark two landmarks to keep the layout lined up (everything turns see-through here). Choose it from the menu each time; after B the app switches to Look only
  • Layouts: save, load, overwrite, or delete named layouts

Right controller

  • Trigger: place / choose · Hold grip + trigger: select · A: done / finish · B: delete the selection, or undo the last corner
  • B: while drawing, removes the last corner. Nothing finished is ever deleted unless it's selected first
  • Stick click with the controller touching the floor: set the floor height

Left controller

  • Trigger: next mode · X: next color · Y: undo (any step; Redo and Snap are in the menu)
  • Stick ↕: height · Stick ↔: opacity
  • ☰: open / close the menu (view options are in there)
  • Hold grip 2s: clear all shapes

Keeping it lined up

Mark A and B on two floor landmarks you can find again (room corners, door frames), far apart. The app pins a spatial anchor at each, and from then on the layout's position and angle come from those two spots plus the floor, so taking the headset off and on doesn't move it. No room scan or boundary is needed. If it ever looks off, mark A and B again at the same spots.
