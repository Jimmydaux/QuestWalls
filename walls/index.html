Home Sketch

Plan a whole home at full size, floor by floor. Press START AR at the bottom. Your work saves automatically; use Layouts to keep named versions. The ☰ Menu button (or M) opens the same menu as in the headset.

Menu (☰ on the left controller)

  • AR / VR at the top. VR: a grey world; walk, or with nothing selected move with the left stick and turn with the right stick
  • ▦ Floor bar: ▼ Down / Up ▲ switch floors, the middle opens Floors: go to a floor, ceiling height, rename, delete, add a floor above (optionally copying the outside walls) or a basement, and show this floor only / floors below faded / the whole house. The floor you're on comes to your feet
  • ▤ Layer: layers, as in Yard Sketch
  • Add: Walls (interior / exterior, height of new walls), Doors & windows, Stairs (straight, L, U with a landing, or draw a path: each turn is a landing), Furniture
  • Tools: New A/B in model, Layouts, Recalibrate floor height
  • 3D view / Plan · 2D: a flat floor plan you can draw on. View: snap to a 1 ft or 6" grid (lines every foot)
  • VR, nothing selected: right stick ↕ raises you for a higher view

Drawing

  • Walls: corners on the floor; click the first corner to close a room (it shows its square feet). A ends a run
  • Doors & windows: point at a wall, trigger one side, then the other (same spot twice = standard width). B on one removes it. Right stick ↕ picks the next type
  • Stairs: ready-made ones put the bottom step at the laser (right stick ↔ turns them); a drawn path starts at the bottom step, trigger at each turn, A finishes. They climb to the next floor
  • Furniture: pick it in the menu; right stick ↔ turns it, ↕ steps through items. B takes back the last one placed

Selecting & editing

  • Right grip, quick press: Select ↔ the last placing mode. Hold the right grip + trigger selects from any mode; grip + B deletes what the laser is on
  • A click opens the edit popup: size, wall / room height, thickness, stair climb and width, turn, raise / lower, opacity, color, layer, floor. Hold the trigger to move it
  • Corners: drag a blue knob · B on a knob removes it · A on the outline adds one (doors and windows stay on their wall)
  • Several at once: hold the left grip and pull the trigger on each (Ctrl+click on a computer)
  • Selected: left stick ↔ width, ↕ depth; right stick ↔ turn, ↕ height. Right stick click switches ↕ to raise / lower

Left controller

  • Trigger: next mode · X: next color · Y: undo · Stick ↕: height · Stick ↔: opacity · Hold grip 2s: clear all (outside Select)

Keeping it lined up

Mark A and B (Reset A/B) on two floor landmarks you can find again (room corners, door frames), far apart. The layout then follows those two spots, so taking the headset off and on doesn't move it. Stand on the real floor that matches the floor you're working on.
