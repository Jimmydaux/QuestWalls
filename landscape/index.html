Yard Sketch

Lay out a landscape at full size in passthrough: retaining walls, patios, boulders, trees and shrubs. Press START AR at the bottom. Work saves automatically; use Layouts for named versions.

Modes (left trigger switches)

  • Retaining walls: points on the ground become a block wall, course by course. Right stick ↕ picks the block, ↔ switches between straight corners and smooth curves, left stick ↕ sets the height. A finishes the wall; aim at the first point and pull the trigger to close it into a raised bed
  • Patios & beds: outline an area (pavers, flagstone, gravel, mulch, lawn, or a swimming pool or pond; lawn and patios get a hole cut around water). Right stick ↕ picks the surface, ↔ switches straight/curved edges. A finishes (3 corners make a rectangle)
  • Library: boulders, blocks, pavers, trees, evergreens, shrubs, grasses, pergolas and pavilions, outdoor furniture. Pick the item from the menu (☰). Right stick ↔ rotates, ↕ lifts it above the ground; left stick ↕ scales it. Items land on top of raised beds and patios. B removes the item you just placed
  • Layers: the blue ▤ Layer link in the menu: move the selection to a layer, make a layer current, show / hide, new, rename, delete
  • Several at once: in Select, hold the left grip and pull the right trigger on each thing (Ctrl+click on a computer). Hold the trigger on one to move them all; right stick ↔ turns the group, left stick sizes it; click one for the group's edit popup (size in all three directions, turn, raise, opacity, delete); B deletes them all
  • Right stick click with something selected: right stick ↕ switches between height and raise / lower
  • AR / VR (top of the menu): VR shows the design on a grey ground under a white sky instead of your yard. Walk around, or move with the left stick when nothing is selected
  • Right grip, quick press: switch between Select and the last placing mode you used
  • Selecting, in any mode: hold the right grip and pull the trigger on something. Or while holding the grip, just point and press B to delete it. After selecting: A = done, B = delete, hold the trigger to move it (right stick ↕ while carrying raises or lowers anything: items, walls, patios, pools), sticks resize and turn it
  • Menu: press the ☰ button on the left controller to open it, press again to close it. It has every choice: mode, wall block, surface, library item, color, view, plus actions for the selected thing. The top line shows what's active. Point the right laser (it turns yellow on the menu) and pull the trigger
  • Select mode: like holding the grip, without holding it. Selected: left stick ↔ width, ↕ depth; right stick ↔ rotate, ↕ height. More actions (new look, next type, curve, raise/lower, opacity) are in the menu
  • Ground slope (menu): make the ground a slope. Pull the trigger on the ground (or touch the controller to it and press A) at 2 or more spots; lawns and patios tilt, walls step down it, items sit on it, pools and ponds stay level. B removes the last spot, right stick ↕ nudges it. The menu's Ground row has Recalibrate height (if things sit too high or low, e.g. after taking the headset off: touch the ground and press A), Reset slope, and Put all on slope (clears every hand raise/lower)
  • Anchor A/B: mark two landmarks to keep the layout lined up. Choose it from the menu each time; after B is marked the app switches to Look only
  • Look only (menu): every button, stick and laser is off, so you can just walk around and look, or hand the headset to someone. ☰ still opens the menu. Handing it over: choose Anchor A/B, let them mark A and B on your landmarks, and they're set to walk around
  • Layouts: save, load, overwrite, or delete named layouts
  • Estimate: block, paver, stone, mulch and plant counts for the whole layout

Left controller

  • Trigger: next mode · X: next color · Y: undo (any step; Redo and Snap are in the menu)
  • Stick ↕: height (or item size) · Stick ↔: opacity
  • ☰: open / close the menu (view options are in there)
  • Hold grip 2s: clear everything

Right controller

  • Trigger: place / choose · Hold grip + trigger: select · A: done / finish · B: delete the selection, or undo the last point
  • Stick click with the controller touching the ground: set the ground height

Outdoors

The Quest finds the floor from its room scan, which usually doesn't exist outside. Touch the right controller to the ground and click the right stick to set it. Mark A and B on things that won't move (a house corner, a fence post) so the layout stays put between sessions.
