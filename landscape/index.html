Yard Sketch

Lay out a landscape at full size in passthrough: retaining walls, patios, boulders, trees and shrubs. Press START AR at the bottom. Work saves automatically; use Layouts for named versions.

Modes (left trigger switches)

  • Retaining walls: points on the ground become a block wall, course by course. Right stick ↕ picks the block, ↔ switches between straight corners and smooth curves, left stick ↕ sets the height. Grip ends the wall, A closes it into a raised bed
  • Patios & beds: outline an area (pavers, flagstone, gravel, mulch, lawn…). Right stick ↕ picks the surface, ↔ switches straight/curved edges. A finishes (3 corners make a rectangle)
  • Library: boulders, blocks, pavers, trees, evergreens, shrubs, grasses. Right stick ↕ picks the item, ↔ rotates, A jumps to the next category, left stick ↕ scales it. B removes the item you just placed
  • Select: trigger selects; hold the trigger to move it. Left stick ↔ width, ↕ depth. Right stick ↔ rotate, ↕ height. Grip changes the block/surface type, or gives a rock or plant a new random shape. A steps opacity, B deletes
  • Anchor A/B: mark two landmarks to keep the layout lined up
  • Layouts: save, load, overwrite, or delete named layouts
  • Estimate: block, paver, stone, mulch and plant counts for the whole layout

Left controller

  • Trigger: next mode · X: next color · Y: snap on/off
  • Stick ↕: height (or item size) · Stick ↔: opacity
  • Stick click: cycle view: everything → menus hidden → clean (no labels either)
  • Hold grip 2s: clear everything

Right controller

  • Trigger: place / select / choose · B: undo the last point
  • Stick click with the controller touching the ground: set the ground height

Outdoors

The Quest finds the floor from its room scan, which usually doesn't exist outside. Touch the right controller to the ground and click the right stick to set it. Mark A and B on things that won't move (a house corner, a fence post) so the layout stays put between sessions.
